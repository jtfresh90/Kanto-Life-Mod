NPCs Immersion Changes What it does: It’s called Kanto Life. 
Just brings it to life more. 
Adds more random NPCs between towns and in towns, each interactable. 
You now will have people kick you out of their houses for entering without knocking. 
You can knock by clicking A on their houses.
This doesn’t apply to marts and poke enters and nonresidential houses. 
You also have a function where NPCs will actually sleep at night.
Someone else can edit to make sprites have a sleeping animation or a bed in their house to sleep in. 
It basically makes them turn on their side and lay down when it’s night, minus quest characters.
Some trainers asleep too, but they will battle with you initiate dialogue with them.
Others will just give dialogue that they’re asleep.
